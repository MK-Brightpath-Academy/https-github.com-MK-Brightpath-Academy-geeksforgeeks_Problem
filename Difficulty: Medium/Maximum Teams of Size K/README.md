<h2><a href="https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/problems/maximum-team-of-k-player/1?page=14&category=Arrays%2CStrings%2CJava%2CLinked%20List&company=Amazon%2CMicrosoft%2CGoogle%2CFlipkart&sortBy=submissions">Maximum Teams of Size K</a></h2><h3>Difficulty Level : Difficulty: Medium</h3><hr><div class="problems_problem_content__Xm_eO" style="--text-color: var(--problem-text-color);"><p><span style="font-size: 18px;">Given an array&nbsp;<strong>arr[]</strong>&nbsp;consisting of&nbsp;<strong>n</strong> positive integers and a positive integer&nbsp;<strong>k</strong>&nbsp;such that there are&nbsp;<strong>n</strong>&nbsp;countries, each country has&nbsp;<strong>arr[i]</strong> players. Find the <strong>maximum</strong> number of teams that can be formed by forming teams of size k&nbsp;such that each player in the team is from a different country.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Examples:</strong></span></p>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input:</strong> arr[] = [4, 3, 5, 3], k = 3
<strong>Output: </strong>5
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Consider the countries are named A, B, C and D. The possible ways of forming the teams are [A, B, C], [A, C, D], [A, B, C], [B, C, D], [A, C, D] such that in each set there are no more than 1 person from a country. Therefore, the total count teams formed is 5.</span></pre>
<pre><span style="font-size: 18px;"><strong>Input: </strong>arr[] = [2, 3, 4], k = 2
<strong>Output: </strong>4
<strong>Explanation: </strong>Consider the countries are named A, B, C and D. The possible ways of forming the teams are {B, C}, {B, C}, {A, C}, ({A, B} or {A, C} or {B, C}) such that in each set there are no more than 1 person from a country. Therefore, the total count teams formed is 4.</span></pre>
